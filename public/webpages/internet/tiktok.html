TikTok

TABLE OF CONTENTS
  • Origin
  • Usage

TikTok is an LA-based and Singaporean-based social media app, most notable for its short-form and vertical videos submitted by users.

Origin

On September 20, 2016, Chinese company ByteDance launched an app called A.me. In December of the same year, it would change its name to Douyin. It spread very quickly and became popular in the country, gaining 100 million users in a year.

ByteDance then planned to launch Douyin overseas in an attempt to beat competitors. This international version was called TikTok, which hit markets in 2017. In the same year, ByteDance purchased Musical.ly, who worked similarly to the TikTok we know now. TikTok and Musical.ly merged into one in 2018, under the former's title.

Starting 2019, many people, common folk and celebrities alike, started to join TikTok. By 2020, 800 million users were on TikTok. This number has continued to grow through the years.

Usage

The app consists of 4 main tabs, a search feature, and a creation feature. The first is called the For You Page, or FYP for short. It is an infinite rotation of videos that have been filtered based on other posts the user has viewed, liked, commented, reposted, or searched for. Videos with similar topics to those you engage with the most often appear on your FYP.

The second is the TikTok shop, where is an online marketplace where users can search for products promoted in videos. The third is the user's inbox, which contains chats with the user's friends and activity that the user has gained, normally via likes, profile viewing, and comments/replies. Finally, we have the profile, where the user can access and modify settings and preferences, as well as see their own videos and analytics.

TikTok also features a search feature for users to find videos (which they can filter based on like count and date). Of course, there is the central feature of video creation, which is a plus button in the center of the taskbar at the bottom. There, users can choose to upload a video/photo, change video duration (10 minutes at maximum), and add filters to their videos.
