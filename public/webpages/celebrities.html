Celebrity of the Day

Sabrina Carpenter

If you've been on TikTok, you've probably seen this trend of comments having their last word capitalized and a purple emoji capping off the comment. While it seems random, it actually came from a Instagram comment from Italian fashion designer and businesswoman Donatella Versace on a pro-Palestine post by model Bella Hadid. Ever since, it has been used in a random manner, with several other memes (often quotes) following the format being deemed "purple heart core". Some example are "what I DO 💜", "catch me outside how bout DAT 💜", and "kate open the GATE 💜".
