Sabrina Carpenter

TABLE OF CONTENTS
  • Career
  • Movies and Television

Sabrina Annlynn Carpenter is an American singer, songwriter, and actress. She is best known for her musical career, especially after the release of her album emails i can't send, where she got widespread recognition for songs such as "Nonsense", "Feather", and "skinny dipping". She is also popular as an actress, most notably for "Girl Meets World" as Maya Hart. She has also made a debut on Broadway for Mean Girls, playing as Cady Heron.

Career

Carpenter was born on May 11, 1999, in Pennsylvania. While she was homeschooled, she had a passion for music, studying voice at the age of 6. She often posted covers of popular songs on her YouTube account. Seeing her passion for music, her father built her her own recording studio. .

Carpenter first received an acting role in 2011, for Law & Order: Special Victims Unit. She also had a recurring role in The Goodwin Games, and voiced Princess Vivian in Sofia the First.

Her first main role appeared with "Girl Meets World" as Maya Hart. The show ran from 2013 to 2017. Carpenter recorded the theme song to the show, and would later sign a five-album deal with Hollywood Records (under Disney).

In 2015, Carpenter released her debut album, Eyes Wide Open, with the lead single "We'll Be the Stars". The album was a folk-pop and teen pop record. It received positive reviews and peaked at 43 on the Billboard Hot 200. The following year, she released her second album, EVOLution, most notable for its single "Thumbs". It was a departure from its predecessor, being more geared towards dance pop. In the same year, she also starred in The Adventures of Babysitting and got a main role in Milo Murphy's Law.

2 years later, she released her third album, Singular: Act I, with singles such as "Sue Me" and "Paris", receiving critical acclaim for its more mature and personal sound compared to her previous 2 albums. The next year, Carpenter released the sequel album, Singular: Act II, giving her her most successful single under Disney's Hollywood Records, "Looking At Me". Later that year, Carpenter would star in the Netflix film Tall Girl. In 2020, Carpenter would perform for the Broadway adaptation of Mean Girls as Cady Heron, but only got to perform in 2 shows due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

In 2021, Carpenter left Hollywood Records and signed with Island Records. In 2022, she released emails i can't send , which garnered her widespread attention. Specifically, her song "Nonsense" became viral due to its outro, which Sabrina often modified and improvised on tour depending on the city she was in. A deluxe version was released with new single "Feather", which garnered virality and controversy over its depiction of multiple men dying and scenes of Carpenter dressed explicitly in a Church. In 2023, she would open for Taylor Swift's The Eras Tour.

Carpenter finally reached true global superstardom with the release of Short n' Sweet. The album received critical acclaim. It contained many hits, both single and nonsingle. Such examples are the lead single "Espresso", "Please Please Please", "Taste", "Bed Chem", "Juno", the latter becoming viral for its "Juno position" tradition from the album's tour. The album also got Carpenter her first two Grammys, for Best Pop Vocal Album and Best Pop Solo Performance ("Espresso"). A deluxe version was released, featuring a remix of "Please Please Please" with Dolly Parton and new single "Busy Woman". Her popularity further increased with 2025's Man's Best Friend, with hit singles "Manchild", "Tears", "When Did You Get Hot?", and "House Tour". The album cover, however, garnered controversy due to depicting Carpenter on her knees with her hair being pulled, similar to a dog on a leash.
