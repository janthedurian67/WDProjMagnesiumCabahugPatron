Rise of the Internet

Internet Term of the Day

TikTok

Unless you've been living under a rock, you probably know what this app is. TikTok is a Singaporean-based and LA-based social media app. It is most famous for its vertical, short-form videos that users can record, edit, and post easily in seconds. TikTok often sports and reflects current trends, such as dance challenges, reaction videos, and even media of shows. According to DemandSage, TikTok has currently 1.99 billion users globally.
